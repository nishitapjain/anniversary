A note from the heart

For the two of you

Twenty-one years is a thousand things: little acts of kindness, familiar smiles, shared jokes, sacrifices nobody saw, and a love that kept showing up.

Twenty-one years ago, you started a story that somehow became our entire world. We get to see what love looks like not just in big gestures, but in the everyday things — in the care, the patience, the laughter, and in Dad treating Mom like his princess, year after year.

Thank you for building a home that we get to grow up in. Thank you for being each other's person, and for being ours, too. We may not say it enough, but so much of who we are comes from the love you've given us. We hope the years ahead bring you even more adventures, silly moments, long conversations, good health, and reasons to fall in love with each other all over again. If we could choose our parents in every lifetime, we'd choose you. Every single time.

Here's to the beautiful life you've built, to the memories that make you smile, and to every new adventure that is yet to come. May the years ahead be just as warm, silly, wonderful, and full of love.

Happy 21st anniversary!

With all our love ♡
